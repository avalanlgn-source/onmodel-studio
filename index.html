How a product runs

  1. You send the garment — on a mannequin, flat, or shot on someone else — plus any model, footwear and setting notes.
  2. I build the scene and place the piece on the agreed model.
  3. You get 4–5 frames per product in one lighting family, ready for a product page.

What holds, and what doesn't

  • Holds: cut, drape, colour, hardware, closures, trims, stripes and blocked colour.
  • Needs a second pass: intricate all-over prints, and any small printed text or logo — legible-but-wrong lettering is the fastest way to lose a customer's trust, so those frames get composited or cut.
  • Quality control: roughly one frame in four is rejected in-house for face drift before anything reaches you.
